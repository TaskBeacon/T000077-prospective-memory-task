# CHANGELOG

## [v0.1.0] - 2026-07-27

### Added

- Implemented the Brewer et al. (2010) baseline, focal, and nonfocal event-based
  prospective-memory paradigm.
- Added deterministic fixed-position cue scheduling, lexical-decision scoring,
  post-response PM detection, false-alarm measures, and ongoing-task cost fields.
- Added Chinese instructions, QA/simulation profiles, an embedded-figures delay,
  literature mappings, and TAPS v0.2.0 metadata.

### Changed

  preserving the published lexicality ratio, targets, casing, and target positions.

### Fixed
